# Pre-registration: simulation v2, person-level testing histories (2026-11-19)

Made with AI agents (Claude) working in the name of MV2 of Munim, Inc. No human expert has
reviewed it. Text CC BY 4.0. Information only, not medical advice. Written and published
**before the simulation has been run even once** (the code is tested only by small unit tests
whose outputs are not results). Simulation v1 (findings/sim-prereg-v1-2026-11-16.md) and
addenda 1-3 are unchanged. Any later change will be a new, dated note.

Code: `kfretransport/sim2.py` (version 0.2.6). Command, fixed now:

    python -m kfretransport.sim2 --reps 200 --n 20000 --seed 20261119 --out-dir out

## Why

Addenda 2 and 3 rest on a model (Gaussian unexplained risk, a single testing threshold) and on
formulas that are exact only on the hazard scale and for rare outcomes. A health system would
run something else: person-level follow-up with censoring, death competing with kidney failure,
Aalen-Johansen observed risk, small bands merged, and a grid fit. This simulation runs **that
code path end to end** (`nested.bands_from_rows`, `nested.fit_within`, `selection.fit_sites`,
`nested.consistency`) on synthetic people and asks three things:

1. Does the within-system test recover a known theta, with honest intervals?
2. Is it quiet when testing carries no information about risk?
3. Addendum 3 says that if people are tested at a rate tied to risk (Poisson recency) rather than
   by a threshold, the within-system theta comes out **smaller** than the cross-site theta. Is
   that true?

For the NIH NCATS Real-World Evidence Innovation Challenge this is the "internal validation"
evidence for the method before any real data are touched.

## Generator (fixed; full detail in the module docstring)

Per person: U ~ N(0,1) (risk the equation misses), x ~ N(0,1) (the equation's own predictor).
Predicted 5-year risk p = 1 - exp(-0.04 exp(0.8 x)) (mean about 5-6%). KRT hazard constant in
time, (H/1826 days) exp(g U - g^2/2). Death hazard -ln(0.8)/1826 per day (20% at 5 years), times
exp(g_d U - g_d^2/2). Follow-up Uniform(365, 3650) days. Horizon 5 years (1826 days).

Testing, windows 90, 180 and 365 days:

| Mechanism | Rule |
|---|---|
| threshold | V = rho U + beta_x x + sqrt(1 - rho^2 - beta_x^2) e; tested within W_j when V > -Phi^-1(s f_j), f = 0.45, 0.70, 1.0, s = the site's 365-day testing share. theta = g rho |
| recency | days since the latest uACR ~ Exponential(r0 exp(kappa U - kappa^2/2)), r0 = -ln(1 - s)/365 |

Eight sites per replicate, s = 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30, 0.40, 0.50, 0.60, 0.70; 20,000 people per
site; 200 replicates per scenario; seed 20261119 (one stream per scenario, seeded "20261119:<name>").

| Scenario | Mechanism | g | rho or kappa | beta_x | g_death | True theta |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T69 | threshold | 0.8 | rho 0.8625 | 0 | 0 | 0.69 (Stockholm's O/E 2.11, if all of it were selection) |
| T30 | threshold | 0.8 | rho 0.375 | 0 | 0 | 0.30 |
| N0 | recency | 0.8 | kappa 0 | - | 0 | 0 (testing unrelated to risk) |
| R69 | recency | 0.8 | kappa 0.8625 | - | 0 | none (not a threshold model; short-window limit exp(g kappa) = exp(0.69)) |
| TX | threshold | 0.8 | rho 0.8625 | 0.3 | 0 | 0.69 nominal; testing also follows the equation's own predictor |
| TD | threshold | 0.8 | rho 0.8625 | 0 | 0.5 | 0.69 nominal; death also tied to U |

## Analysis (fixed)

Per site: `nested.bands_from_rows` at 5 years (bands 0-90, 91-180, 181-365 days; Aalen-Johansen
O/E with death competing; delta-method variance; bands with < 20 events merged). The 365-day
tested group's Aalen-Johansen O/E. The same three bands with 1 - Kaplan-Meier (death censored).

Per replicate: theta_W = `nested.fit_within` over all usable sites (one intercept per site;
grid [-1, 3], step 0.001; 95% profile interval). theta_B = `selection.fit_sites` on the sites'
365-day tested groups (rate = observed share tested; tau^2 = 0). z = `nested.consistency`.
Misfit = q_min above the chi-squared 95% point with (bands - sites - 1) df (Wilson-Hilferty).

## Predictions (graded by `sim2.grade`; this text is the authority)

- **P1 (recovery, theta 0.69):** in T69, mean theta_W within 0.10 of 0.69 **and** 95%-interval
  coverage of 0.69 between 0.85 and 0.99.
- **P2 (recovery, theta 0.30):** in T30, mean theta_W within 0.10 of 0.30 and coverage between
  0.85 and 0.99.
- **P3 (no false alarms):** in N0, the share of replicates whose theta_W interval excludes 0 is
  at most 0.08, and mean theta_W is within 0.05 of 0.
- **P4 (addendum 3's mechanism claim):** in R69, mean theta_W < mean theta_B, and theta_W <
  theta_B in at least 80% of replicates.
- **P5 (the consistency check is not trigger-happy):** in T69 and in T30, |z| > 1.96 in at most
  10% of replicates.
- **P6 (cross-site recovery):** in T69 and T30, mean theta_B within 0.10 of the true theta and
  coverage between 0.85 and 0.99.
- **P7 (direction of the known approximation error):** in T69, mean theta_W < 0.69 (the curve
  is exact for hazards; risks saturate and death competes, which should shrink the gradient), and
  in TD mean theta_W is lower still than in T69 (death tied to U removes high-risk people before
  kidney failure).

Expectation stated before running, so it can be checked: a rough hand estimate puts the risk-
scale and competing-death attenuation in T69 at a few hundredths (perhaps 0.03-0.07). If the
interval is tight (pooled standard error near 0.04), that much bias could pull P1's coverage
under 0.85 even when the mean passes. A P1 failure on coverage alone would mean that the
production analysis needs a risk-scale correction before real data. It would not mean the design
is wrong.

## Descriptive outputs (no prediction)

- D1: mean theta_W with 1 - Kaplan-Meier bands next to Aalen-Johansen, every scenario.
- D2: bias of theta_W and theta_B in TX (testing that also follows the equation's predictor).
- D3: bias in TD.
- D4: in R69, the misfit share and the consistency-rejection share, i.e. whether the band shape
  or the N3 check can tell recency from threshold testing at this size.
- D5: mean usable sites, interval-at-grid-edge share, mean band O/E per site next to the
  closed-form band factors.

## How results will be read

| Outcome | Reading |
|---|---|
| P1-P3 pass | the within-system test does what addendum 3 says on realistic person-level data |
| P1 or P2 fail on coverage only | add a risk-scale correction (selection.risk_scale_factors) to the production fit, in a dated addendum, before any real data |
| P3 fails | the within-system test is not usable as specified; addendum 3's N1 would need a new null calibration |
| P4 fails | addendum 3's mechanism sentence is wrong and will be corrected publicly |
| P5 fails | N3 must not be read as evidence for one selection parameter; a bootstrap version would be needed |

All results, including failures, will be published in this repository.
